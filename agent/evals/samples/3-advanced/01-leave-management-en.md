### Example 5 - Advanced level
```
I want to manage a leave management application for SMEs.

Context:
My company has 10 meeting rooms.

Business rules:
- 25 days of paid leave per year
- Leave by half day or full day
- 15 days notice recommended
- Maximum 3 people on leave at the same time in a team

Required operations:
- Employee: request leave, view their balance
- Manager: approve/reject
- HR: manage balances
- Email notifications on approvals.
```
