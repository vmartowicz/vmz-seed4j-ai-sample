### Example 3 - Intermediate level
```
I want to build a parking management system for my residential building.

Context:
My building has 10 visitor parking spaces that residents can book for their guests.

Business rules:
- A resident can book at most 2 spaces per month
- Bookings can be made up to 7 days in advance
- A booking is for a full day (from 00:00 to 23:59)
- A resident must provide their name, the space number and the date
- There are 10 visitor spaces available
- A space cannot be booked twice for the same date
- Residents can cancel their booking up to 24 hours before

Required operations:
- Create a booking
- Cancel a booking
- View the available spaces for a given date
- See all my current bookings
```
