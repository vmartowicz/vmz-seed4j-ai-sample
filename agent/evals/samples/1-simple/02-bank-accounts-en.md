### Example 1 - Simple level
```
Banking application

Context:
I want to manage bank acounts.

Business rules:
- an account can receive transfers
- an account can make payments
- the balance of an account cannot be negative
- Each operation generates an audit event

Required operations:
- Open an account
- View the balance of an account
- Receive a transfer on an account
- Make a payment from an account
- View the operation history of an account
```
