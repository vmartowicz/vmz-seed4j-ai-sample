### Example 10 - Expert level
```
I organise "RhôneConf", a yearly tech conference in Lyon: 2 days in October, 1,200 attendees, 5 rooms, about sixty talks. Until now we did everything with online forms, spreadsheets and lots of emails, and last year it ended badly: speakers without a ticket, a workshop with 60 people for 30 seats, a Gold sponsor forgotten on the programme...

I want an application covering the whole organisation. Each volunteer team has its own way of working and its own words, I describe them team by team.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. THE PROGRAMME COMMITTEE (the call for papers)

- The call for papers is open from February 1st to March 31st
- A proposal: title, abstract, format (TALK of 45 min, QUICKIE of 15 min, WORKSHOP of 2 h), level (beginner, intermediate, expert), track (Back, Front, Data, Ops, Craft), one or two speakers
- A speaker can submit 3 proposals at most
- Each proposal is rated from 1 to 5 by at least 3 committee members. A member doesn't rate a proposal from a speaker of their own company
- We accept the best averages within the available slots. In case of a tie, we favour speakers who never spoke at RhôneConf
- Accepted speakers have 10 days to confirm, otherwise we move to the next proposal on the waiting list
- An accepted speaker gets a free ticket

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. THE PROGRAMME TEAM (the schedule of the 2 days)

- 5 rooms: Amphi (500 seats), Saône (200), Rhône (200), Fourvière (80, for workshops), Croix-Rousse (40)
- A day: from 9:00 to 18:00, 40-minute slots with a 10-minute break between two slots, lunch break from 12:30 to 14:00
- The opening keynote takes place in the Amphi on the first day at 9:00, nothing else at the same time
- A speaker can't be in two rooms at the same time, and a speaker coming from far away (outside France) doesn't speak on the first day before 11:00
- Workshops only take place in the Fourvière room and take 2 consecutive slots
- We don't put two talks of the same track at the same time
- If a speaker cancels, their slot is freed and we offer it to the next proposal on the waiting list

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. TICKETING

- Prices: EARLY BIRD €249 (until April 30th or the first 300 tickets), STANDARD €349, STUDENT €99 (with proof), free ticket for speakers and sponsors
- 10% discount from 5 tickets bought in a single order (companies)
- Tickets are not refundable, but the attendee name can be changed up to 7 days before the conference
- Full refund when cancelling up to 30 days before
- Workshops require a registration (with a ticket), 30 seats per workshop, waiting list when full
- We don't sell more tickets than seats: 1,200 attendees in total, speakers and sponsors included

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. SPONSORING

Packages:
- GOLD: €8,000, 6 m² booth, logo on the Amphi stage, 4 tickets included, a 40-min sponsored talk
- SILVER: €4,000, 4 m² booth, 2 tickets included
- BRONZE: €1,500, logo on the website, 1 ticket included

Rules:
- 3 GOLD at most, 8 SILVER at most, no limit for BRONZE
- A package is reserved on signature, and confirmed on payment. Without payment within 30 days, the package is released
- Booths are assigned in payment order, GOLD sponsors choose first
- The sponsored talk goes through the programme committee like the others, but it is accepted automatically
- GOLD sponsors have 5 badges for their booth team

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. ON-SITE WELCOME (badges and rooms)

- Each attendee gets a badge on arrival, on presentation of their ticket. The badge shows its type: ATTENDEE, SPEAKER, SPONSOR, VOLUNTEER
- A ticket only gives one badge. If the name changed, we print the new name
- At the room entrances, badges are scanned to count the people inside. When a room is full, entry is refused and people are sent to the live stream in the hall
- For workshops, only registered attendees can enter. Others are let in within the room's seats
- We want to know in real time how full each room is, and at the end the most attended talks

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 WHAT I WANT FIRST

1. The call for papers and the talk selection
2. Ticket sales and workshop registrations
3. The schedule
4. Badges and room counting
5. Sponsors

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❓ THE QUESTIONS MY TEAMS ASKED ME

- The committee: "When an accepted speaker withdraws, do we pick the replacement or does the programme team?"
- Ticketing: "A speaker who already bought their ticket before being accepted, do we refund them?"
- The welcome desk: "A sponsor who comes to give a talk, which badge do we give them?"
```
