### Example 9 - Expert level
```
I run "FitCity", a network of 4 gyms (Lyon Part-Dieu, Lyon Confluence, Villeurbanne, Écully), about 3,000 members. Today each team manages its own spreadsheets and nothing is consistent anymore: a member who cancelled can still get in, a cancelled class stays booked, accounting doesn't know who is suspended...

I want a single application, but each team has its own needs and its own vocabulary. I describe them team by team.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

1. THE SALES TEAM (memberships)

Plans:
- ESSENTIAL: €29.90/month, access to a single gym (the home gym), 4 group classes per month
- PREMIUM: €49.90/month, access to the 4 gyms, unlimited classes
- STUDENT: -30% on ESSENTIAL, with a valid student card
- 12-month commitment, or no commitment (+€10/month)
- Sign-up fee: €39 (free in January and September)

Rules:
- Members must be at least 16 (parental consent between 16 and 18)
- The plan can be changed once a month, the change takes effect the next month
- Cancellation with a one-month notice. Committed members can't cancel before the end of the 12 months, except when moving or for medical reasons (with proof)
- Medical suspension: from 1 to 3 months, once a year. Nothing is paid during the suspension and the commitment is extended accordingly
- Referral: the referrer gets a free month when the referred member has paid their 2nd monthly fee

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

2. THE COACHES (the schedule)

- Each gym has studios: 3 at Part-Dieu, 2 in the others. Each studio has a capacity (from 12 to 30 people)
- Classes: yoga, pilates, cycling, cross-training, boxing... A class lasts 45 min or 1 h, has a coach, a studio and a capacity that doesn't exceed the studio's
- The schedule is published every week, classes repeat from one week to the next
- A coach doesn't teach more than 25 hours a week and needs 15 minutes between two classes (30 minutes when changing gyms)
- A coach must be certified in the discipline they teach
- If a coach is sick, we look for a certified replacement, otherwise the class is cancelled

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

3. THE MEMBERS (class bookings)

- A class can be booked at most 7 days before, and up to 1 h before it starts
- An ESSENTIAL member only books in their gym, within their quota (2 classes per week)
- If the class is full, members can join a waiting list. When a spot frees up, the first one on the list takes it automatically and gets a notification
- Free cancellation up to 2 h before. After that, or when the member doesn't come, it's a "no-show". 3 no-shows in 30 days = no booking for 7 days
- If the gym cancels a class, all attendees are notified and the class doesn't count in their quota

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

4. ACCOUNTING (billing)

- Monthly direct debit on the 5th of each month
- The first month is billed pro rata of the remaining days, plus the sign-up fee
- If a direct debit fails: new attempt 3 days later, then 7 days later. After 3 failures the member is in arrears: their access is blocked until they pay and a €10 fee is added
- After 2 months in arrears, the membership is terminated and the file goes to debt collection
- We want the monthly revenue per gym (the member's home gym)
- Classes cancelled by the gym are not refunded, except as a goodwill gesture from the gym manager (a credit note on the next invoice)

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

5. THE FRONT DESK (access control)

- Each member has a badge. At the turnstile, entry is accepted if the membership is active, paid, and gives access to this gym
- A suspended member or a member in arrears is refused with a message to go to the front desk
- The same badge can't enter twice without exiting, except after 4 h
- Lost badge: €10 for a new one, the old one is deactivated immediately
- A PREMIUM member can bring a guest twice a month, the guest is registered at the front desk
- We want to know in real time how many people are in each gym (150 maximum at Part-Dieu, 100 in the others). When the gym is full, entries are refused

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

🎯 WHAT I WANT FIRST

1. Subscribe to a membership and pay the first invoice
2. Enter a gym with a badge
3. Book a class, with the waiting list
4. Manage arrears
5. The coaches' schedule

The rest can wait for a second version.

═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

❓ THE QUESTIONS MY TEAMS ASKED ME

- The coaches: "When a class is cancelled, is it up to us to notify the members?"
- Accounting: "A member on medical suspension, do we still debit them on the 5th?"
- The front desk: "An ESSENTIAL member who booked a class in another gym, do we let them in?"
```
